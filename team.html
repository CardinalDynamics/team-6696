Our team

We're a student-led FIRST Robotics Competition team at Corbett High School in Corbett, Oregon, at the mouth of the scenic Columbia River Gorge. Since 2017, our students have designed, built, and programmed robots while learning how to solve problems together.

Robotics in the classroom

FRC is part of the curriculum at Corbett High School through an elective robotics course. Students apply what they learn in class to mechanical design, CAD, programming, electronics, and competition strategy.

Each January, a new FIRST Robotics Competition game starts another cycle of design, building, testing, and improvement. Our mentors help students develop the skills to turn their ideas into a working robot.

Every student has a real role and a chance to contribute, including marketing, social media, fundraising, and project management. Teamwork, communication, and learning from mistakes matter just as much as the robot we bring to the field.

Who can join?

Students who attend Corbett High School or live within the Corbett School District boundaries are welcome to join. Contact our coaches to learn how to get involved.

Do I need robotics experience?

You can start with curiosity and a willingness to learn. Students work together across design, building, programming, and strategy with support from mentors.

When does the team meet?

Contact the coaches for the current class and meeting schedule. Build and competition season will involve a significant time commitment outside regular class.

Can adults get involved?

Yes. We welcome interest from technical and non-technical mentors. Email the team to talk about your interests and availability.
