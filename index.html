Who We Are

Built by
Students.

Cardinal Dynamics is a FIRST Robotics Competition (FRC) team based at Corbett High School in Corbett, Oregon. We were founded in 2017 and operate as an optional class — open to any student who's ready to tackle engineering challenges.

Every year, FIRST releases a new game challenge in January, and teams have just 6 weeks to design, build, program, and test an industrial-size robot. We compete against thousands of other high school teams from across the globe.

We're a small but mighty team — students handle everything from mechanical design and CAD to software and strategy. Every member has a real job, real responsibility, and real impact.
