A new chapter for Tiara Friday: #FIRSTLikeAGirl ambassadors

Cardinal Dynamics has been selected as a 2026-2027 #FIRSTLikeAGirl Ambassador Team, bringing new support to our Tiara Friday outreach and our message of supporting women in STEM.

Cardinal Dynamics has been selected to represent #FIRSTLikeAGirl as a 2026-2027 Ambassador Team. We're excited to join a community that encourages girls and women to pursue STEM and find their place in FIRST.

This ambassadorship will help us grow our Tiara Friday outreach and share our message of supporting women in STEM throughout the PNW and beyond. We want students to see that they belong, that their ideas matter, and that there are people ready to support them.
