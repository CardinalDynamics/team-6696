S.A.M. goes to Worlds

From Wilsonville and Clackamas to the FIRST World Championships, S.A.M. took Team 6696 through a memorable REBUILT season.

Our 2026 robot, S.A.M., competed at Wilsonville, Clackamas, the PNW District Championships, and the FIRST World Championships in Houston.

The robot was designed, built, and programmed by Cardinal Dynamics students. The season brought together mechanical design, programming, strategy, and plenty of teamwork.

Thank you to the students, mentors, families, and supporters who made the season possible.
